I'm a Design Engineer at Adobe. Here are a few projects I've worked on.

## Premiere Color Mode

I helped prototype Color Mode, Premiere's color-grading tools for video editors.

![Premiere Color Mode showing a before-and-after color grade and color controls](/images/work/premiere-color-mode.webp)

[See Color Mode on Adobe.com](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/color-mode.html)

---

## Firefly Design

I prototyped motion and agentic motion for Firefly Design, Adobe's new graphic design tool, porting its C++/WebAssembly engine into a prototype front end focused on animation.

![Firefly Design showing an Animate this text control on a selected graphic](/images/work/firefly-design-motion.webp)

[See Firefly Design on Adobe.com](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/features/design.html)

---
