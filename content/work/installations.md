Installations I've worked on for museums and galleries.

## AI Will Replace U

An installation I built for Sun Gallery using Stable Diffusion, TensorFlow.js body segmentation, and Lit/Web Components. Visitors describe what AI should replace them with, and the installation generates an image in their place.

![An example image generated by the AI Will Replace U installation](/images/work/ai-will-replace-u.webp)

## Superheroes

For the de Youngsters Art Party at the de Young Museum, we created a pop-up experience where kids could draw their own superhero, see it on screen, and take home a print.

![The Superheroes pop-up installation at the de Young Museum](/images/work/superheroes.webp)

## Note to Self

An installation we created for the Museum of Photographic Arts, combining visitor portraits with patterns made by sound waves in water. A MIDI keyboard let visitors trigger the sounds.

![The Note to Self sound and photography installation](/images/work/note-to-self.webp)

[Read about Note to Self](/blog/2019-02-01-notetoself/)
